Why review your subscriptions

A subscription pays itself every month, without you thinking about it. That’s convenient, and it’s also why they pile up: a forgotten free trial, a gym you no longer go to, two streaming services for a single series… Each amount looks small, but the yearly total often comes as a surprise.

yearly cost = sum of monthly subscriptions × 12

The 3-step method

  • List everything: go through your last 2 or 3 bank or card statements and spot the payments that come back.
  • Ask one question for each: did I use it last month? Would I sign up again today at this price?
  • Cancel or renegotiate: cut the ones you no longer use, and once a year compare the useful contracts (internet, phone plan, insurance): offers change often.

Example: 2 forgotten subscriptions at 10 and 15 a month add up to 300 a year, or 3,000 over 10 years.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find all my subscriptions?

Go through your bank and card statements for the last few months and spot the recurring payments, monthly or yearly. On your phone, app subscriptions are listed in the App Store or Google Play settings.

Is it easy to cancel?

It depends on the service and on where you live. Many countries have rules that make online subscriptions easier to cancel, but the exact conditions (notice period, minimum term, renewal date) depend on each contract: check its terms before cancelling.

What can I do with the money I save?

Set up an automatic transfer of the same amount to your savings, on the day the subscription used to be charged: you won’t feel the difference. Many people start with an emergency fund.
