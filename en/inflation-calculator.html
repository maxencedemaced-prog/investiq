What inflation does to your savings

Inflation is the general rise in prices. With 2% inflation a year, 10,000 kept as cash only buys about what 6,700 buys today after 20 years. The number on your account stays the same; what it can buy shrinks.

Real return

Your real return is roughly your return minus inflation. A savings account paying 3% with 2% inflation grows your purchasing power by about 1% a year. A return below inflation means you lose purchasing power, even if the balance goes up.

Frequently asked questions

Which inflation rate should I use?

Central banks in the US, the euro area and the UK target around 2% a year over the long run, but actual inflation can be much higher for a while. Try several assumptions.

Is the return guaranteed?

No. Returns are hypothetical; investments can lose value. This calculator is educational and not investment advice.
