What is an emergency fund?

It’s money set aside for the unexpected — a job loss, a car repair, a medical bill — kept somewhere safe and available at any time. Having one means you never have to sell investments at a bad moment.

How many months of expenses?

A common rule of thumb is 3 to 6 months of essential expenses, more if your income is irregular, if you’re the only earner or if you have dependents. The calculator suggests a range based on your situation; it is a starting point, not a rule.

Where do people keep it?

  • United States: often a high-yield savings account or money market account (check FDIC or NCUA insurance).
  • European Union: an easy-access savings account (deposits are protected up to 100,000 per bank under EU deposit guarantee rules).
  • United Kingdom: an easy-access savings account or Cash ISA (FSCS protection up to the current limit).

Frequently asked questions

Should I invest before my emergency fund is ready?

Many people build the cushion first, then invest what they won’t need for several years. Your situation may differ — this is general information, not personal advice.
