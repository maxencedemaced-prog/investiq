What is an MSCI World ETF?

The MSCI World index covers roughly 1,400–1,500 large and mid-sized companies in about 23 developed countries, with a large weight in US companies. An ETF tracking it gives broad diversification in a single purchase. Investors in the US often use similar broad index funds (for example total-market or S&P 500 funds); in Europe, UCITS ETFs tracking the MSCI World or FTSE All-World are common.

Why three scenarios?

The index’s long-term historical average has been in the mid-to-high single digits per year with dividends reinvested, but with large drops along the way (for example around −40% in 2008). The scenarios at 4%, 6% and 8% are hypothetical illustrations, not a forecast. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.
